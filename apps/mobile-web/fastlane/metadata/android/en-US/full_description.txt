Littlelight is a private photo album for your baby's first years. Add your photos, and Littlelight keeps them organised by your baby's age so you can find any moment later without scrolling for hours.

PRIVATE BY DEFAULT
- Nothing is ever public. Every photo is private to you and the people you invite.
- No ads. No tracking. We never sell your data.
- Your photos are never used to train AI models.
- No face recognition.

A CALM TIMELINE
- Your photos grouped by your baby's age: first week, first month, six months and so on.
- Photo-first design with large, quiet grids and a full-screen viewer.
- Add photos from your library or take one with the camera. Uploads continue in the background.
- Import your existing photos from Google Photos.
- Add milestones, measurements and notes to any photo.

FIND ANY MOMENT
- Search in your own words: "first smile", "grandma holding the baby", "beach in June".
- Ask questions about your album and get answers with the matching photos.

SHARE WITH FAMILY, ON YOUR TERMS
- Send grandparents a private link to a hand-picked album. Links expire automatically and you can turn them off at any time.
- Relatives can view without making an account. Anything you have not chosen to share stays hidden.
- Invite a co-parent to manage the album with you, or a nanny or grandparent who can add photos for you to approve.

YOU STAY IN CONTROL
- Delete a photo or your whole account from inside the app.
- Photos travel over encrypted connections, and the location stored in photos is encrypted and never shown to people you share with.

ABOUT AI
Optional AI features use our model provider to describe your photos so you can search them and ask questions about them. Photos are only analysed when AI features are turned on for an album. Your photos are never used to train models, and we delete the files from the provider once the descriptions are made.

Littlelight is for parents and guardians aged 18 and over.
